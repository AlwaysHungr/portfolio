Thank you for your work on children’s rights! Now, you have a clearer idea of the dreams and ambitions people your age have. You can understand how similarly teenagers think and how important preserving children’s rights is to offer equal opportunities and a better future for all.

Υπόδειγμα επιστολής

Dear Adults,

My name is Kleio, I am 15 years old, and I am a student from Greece. Like many teenagers, I have dreams for my future. I would like to study at university, find a job that I love and have the opportunity to travel and learn about different cultures. However, I also dream of a world where every child has the same opportunities, no matter where they are born.

Unfortunately, children in countries affected by war or poverty often cannot enjoy their basic rights. Therefore, governments and international organisations should make sure that every child has access to education, healthcare, safety and protection. For example, schools should be rebuilt in areas affected by war, while children from poor families should receive support so that they can continue their education. Moreover, adults should listen to children and respect their opinions.

In my opinion, if we work together and fight discrimination and inequality, we can create a more peaceful and fair world. Every child deserves to grow up safely and follow their dreams.

Yours faithfully,
Kleio
