Συνδετικές λέξεις και φράσεις

COVID-19 and Education: Your Voice Matters

Choose ONE of the following writing tasks. Use information and evidence from the video and the online resources you explored. Before submitting your work, complete the self-check to make sure you have included all the required elements.

Option 1

Report

Write a report describing the effects of the pandemic on education and suggesting practical solutions. Use evidence from the video and the online resources you read to support your ideas. Write 100–150 words. Use formal, objective language, in the third person where possible. Your text should include:

  • a short introduction to the topic
  • a main body describing 2–3 problems, supported by evidence from the sources
  • at least one practical solution for each problem
  • a clear conclusion
  • appropriate linking words (firstly, furthermore, however, in conclusion, etc.)
Option 2

Formal Letter

Write a formal letter of complaint to the Ministry of Education. Describe the problems students faced during the pandemic and suggest practical solutions to improve education in similar situations in the future. Write 100–150 words. Use formal language, and avoid contractions (like don’t or can’t) and informal expressions. Sign your letter as George Papadakis. Your letter should include:

  • an appropriate opening (e.g. Dear Sir/Madam)
  • the reason for writing the letter
  • description of 1–2 specific problems, with an example or evidence from the sources
  • a polite, clearly stated request or proposal for change
  • an appropriate closing (e.g. Yours faithfully)
Option 3

Social Media Post

Write a short, engaging social media post about an important challenge students faced during the pandemic. Encourage people to think about the issue and support positive change. Write 100–150 words. Use a direct, engaging but respectful tone. Your post should include:

  • a short, engaging opening line that grabs attention
  • description of 1–2 specific problems, with an example or evidence from the sources
  • a call to action or a suggestion addressed to readers
  • 1–2 relevant hashtags (e.g. #StudentWellbeing #EducationMatters)
