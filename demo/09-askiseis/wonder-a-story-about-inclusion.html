Reading text

Auggie Pullman is a ten-year-old boy who was born with a facial difference. Because of the many medical treatments he needed, he has never been to a mainstream school before. Now, for the very first time, he is starting fifth grade at Beecher Prep, a school in New York. Auggie’s wish is simple: he wants to be treated like any other student. However, his new classmates notice his face before anything else about him, and find it hard to look past his appearance. On the inside, though, Auggie is a completely ordinary boy who likes the same things other children like. The big question is whether his classmates will be able to accept him for who he truly is. The story of Auggie, told through his own eyes and later through the eyes of his family and classmates, became an international bestseller and inspired real conversations, and even a movement, about kindness and acceptance in schools around the world.
