«Imagine that you are a travel blogger! Click on the link to visit a site with information about places all around the world. Search for information about a place that you want to visit and write some useful tips and important information about this place. Start by searching for the name of the place and, then, add keywords to get more specific results. Check out the tips below for extra help! Think of these questions when looking around the site for information on the place you chose.

  • Where is it?
  • What are 3 popular sights?
  • What can visitors do there?
  • What food is it famous for?
  • What is interesting about its culture?
  • When is a good time to visit it?
  • What makes it different from other destinations?

When you’re done, you can grade your classmates’ posts with stars!»
