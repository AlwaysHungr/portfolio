Στάση 04 · Reading

Read the following text that discusses various benefits and drawbacks of travelling. Pay attention to what it says, as you can use it to get ideas about your own travel podcast which you will record in the next part of the activity:

Reading lens
Free Time and Travelling Free time is an important part of a balanced lifestyle because it helps people relax, learn new skills, and spend time with others. Many people enjoy activities such as reading, playing sports, listening to music, or travelling. Travelling, in particular, offers many benefits. It allows people to explore new places, experience different cultures, and improve valuable skills such as communication, problem-solving, and independence. For example, a student visiting Italy may learn about its history while practising English with other travellers. However, travelling also has some challenges. It can be expensive, especially during busy seasons, and planning a trip often takes time and careful organisation. In addition, frequent air travel can have a negative impact on the environment. Despite these difficulties, travelling remains a rewarding experience that creates lasting memories and broadens people's perspectives.
